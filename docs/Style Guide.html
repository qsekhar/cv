Section 01

Brand Principles

§01

Four ideas govern every visual and verbal decision. When in doubt, choose the option that more strongly expresses one of these — and never one that contradicts another.

Editorial, not corporate

Design like a thoughtful publication, not a SaaS landing page. Generous whitespace, considered typography, content-first hierarchy.

Engineering signal

Monospaced labels and reference numbers (§01, SSM-2026-014) communicate precision. The system is built, not decorated.

Warm, not cold

Paper background, warm accent, off-white type on navy — never pure white on black. Calm, confident, human.

Restraint

One serif, one sans, one mono. One navy, one accent. No gradients. No emoji. No iconography unless functional.

Clear space

Reserve clear space equal to the cap-height of the wordmark on every side. The mark and wordmark sit on a shared baseline with a 14px gap between them.

Minimum size

Wordmark: 18px / 14pt. Mark alone: 24px square. Below this, legibility breaks down — use the mark alone or the wordmark, never both, at small sizes.

Section 03

Color System

§03

Two anchors (Navy, Paper), one warm accent, and a small neutral set. The accent is rare — used for kickers, section numbers, and small emphasis only. Never as a primary surface.

Pairing rules

Navy + Paper is the canonical pairing. Accent appears only on small marks — never as a fill on areas larger than a button. Ink for body type on Paper. Paper for body type on Navy.

What's banned

No gradients. No drop shadows on type. No pure black (#000) or pure white (#fff) — always warm. No additional accent hues; if you need a second signal, use weight or scale instead.

Pairing

Fraunces for everything that says this is a thought. Inter for everything that says this is a fact you can read. JetBrains Mono for everything that says this is a label or a reference. Never mix display weight serif inside body copy.

Letter-spacing

Fraunces tracks tight: −0.015em on headings, −0.02em on display. Inter at 0. Mono at +0.14em → +0.20em when uppercased as a kicker. Tracking is part of the system, not optional.

Section 05

Spacing & Layout

§05

Spacing follows a 4px base. Most surfaces use 8 / 12 / 18 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 64. Print pages use 18mm margins. Web pages use 48px gutters at desktop, 24px at mobile.

Borders & rules

1px hairline in --line (#C9BFAE). 2px section accent in Navy. Dashed dividers (1px dashed line) for inside-list separators. No rounded corners — corners are square (border-radius: 0).

Grids

Two-column for paired content. Three-column for triplets (stats, options, cards). Four-column for swatches. Gutters: 14–18px. Avoid five+ column grids.

Section 07

Voice & Copy

§07

Direct, concrete, slightly editorial. Short sentences alongside one longer one. Confident without being cold. Technical terms used precisely — never as decoration.

Do

"Three calendar weeks from kickoff. The 48-hour estimate already includes QA and a 20% risk buffer."

Don't

"We'll leverage cutting-edge solutions to deliver world-class results that delight stakeholders."

Do

"The app does only what it needs to — nothing more."

Don't

"Our innovative platform empowers seamless end-to-end integration capabilities."

Sentence rhythm

Mix short and long. A short declarative followed by one with a clause. Avoid three medium sentences in a row — it reads flat.

Numbers & units

Always specific: "136 h", "17 working days", "$2,040". Never "a few weeks" or "around $2k". Specificity is the voice.

Em dashes

Used liberally to qualify a statement — like this. Set with spaces around them. They're part of the editorial tone.

Section 09

Don't

§09
Don't

Use gradients on backgrounds, buttons, or text. The system is flat by design.

Don't

Round corners. Buttons, cards, badges, images — all square.

Don't

Use emoji as design elements. If a glyph is needed, use a typographic mark (·, →, §).

Don't

Introduce a third color hue. If you need contrast, change weight or size, not color.

Don't

Use Inter or any sans for headlines. Headlines are always Fraunces.

Don't

Mix paragraph copy in monospace. Mono is for labels, references, and inline code only.
