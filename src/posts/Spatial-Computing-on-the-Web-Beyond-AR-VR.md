# Spatial Computing on the Web: Beyond AR/VR

Spatial computing is evolving beyond traditional augmented and virtual reality to encompass a broader range of 3D interaction paradigms that are becoming accessible through standard web browsers.

## 1. The Evolution of Web-Based 3D

Modern browsers support increasingly sophisticated 3D capabilities through:
- WebXR for immersive experiences
- WebGL and WebGPU for high-performance graphics
- Web Audio API for spatial audio
- Sensor APIs for motion and orientation tracking

## 2. New Interaction Paradigms

Spatial computing on the web enables:
- Gesture-based navigation without special hardware
- Voice-controlled 3D environments
- Eye-tracking for natural selection and focus
- Haptic feedback through device vibration
- Mixed reality overlays on real-world camera feeds

## 3. Applications Beyond Gaming

Industries are adopting web-based spatial computing for:
- Virtual showrooms and product visualization
- Collaborative design and engineering
- Educational simulations and training
- Medical visualization and diagnosis
- Remote assistance and maintenance

## Conclusion

Spatial computing on the web is democratizing 3D interaction technologies, making them accessible to anyone with a web browser and opening up new possibilities for immersive digital experiences.
